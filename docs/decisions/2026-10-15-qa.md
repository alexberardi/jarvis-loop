# The QA lane: after merge, opt-in

2026-10-15 · item #15. **The user's decision:** manual QA happens after merge and only when asked
for. Some changes can only be checked by the user on their own phone or hardware, and the mobile app
reaches TestFlight and Play only after merge. What was chosen, each enforced by a test or a scenario
(`scenarios/33-qa.json`).

## The stage

- **`qa`, between land and done** (`pipeline.QA`). Land's `merged` moves an item to `qa` instead of
  `done` when it is marked `needs_qa`; otherwise to `done` as before. It is **not a work stage**:
  nothing claims it (`TestQAStage`, the claim table test, `TestSetQAThenMergedGoesToQA`).
- **Who marks it** (`needs_qa`, a column on the item, migration `0005_qa.sql`):
  - triage's `reproduced` and review's `approved` may carry `needs_qa: true` in their result (the
    briefs say when); a later `false` never clears a mark;
  - the user, before the item lands: `/loop qa <id>`, `loop qa <id>`, `POST /items/{id}/qa` (event
    `qa_marked`). Refused (`wrong_stage`) on qa, done and closed: after the merge it is too late;
  - **the repo's default:** `qa: true` in the runner's repo config (`RepoConfig.QA`). The land script
    sends `needs_qa: true` with `merged` for such a repo, and loopd puts the item in qa. Chosen over
    a flag in loopd's `--repos-file` because the runner's config is already where per-repo settings
    live (verify steps, groups, aliases), and the land script is what reports the merge. A repo's
    default cannot be turned off for one item; Verified is one press.
- **Verified** (button, `/loop verified <id>`, `loop verified`, `POST /items/{id}/verified`): qa →
  done, event `verified`.
- **Reopen with words** (`/loop reopen <id> <words>`, `loop reopen <id> "<words>"`,
  `POST /items/{id}/reopen`, or the button then a reply): in one transaction, a **new P2 item** in
  triage, same repo, the words as its ask, titled "Reopen #N: …", linked by `reopen_of` (shown on
  its Slack parent as "Reopens #N" and in `loop show`); this item qa → done, event `reopened` with
  the new item's id. The merged work is not reverted: the fix is new work. No words is refused
  (`missing_field`).
- **Drop** still works on a qa item (→ closed); priority can still change. **main's CI** on the
  merge commit is recorded on a qa item (and on one verified, reopened or dropped since), as on a done
  one: the merge is what it watches.

## Slack

- `merged` into qa posts **"<@approver> #N: Merged — waiting for your check."** with the PR link and
  merge commit, and **Verified** / **Reopen** buttons. Only the approver may press them; anyone
  else's press is refused and recorded (`refused`), the same check as Approve and Drop.
- **Reopen needs words, and a button cannot take any.** Reopen therefore asks in the thread: "Reply
  in this thread with what is wrong …", and the approver's **next reply in that thread** is the
  words. A reply on a qa item before Reopen is pressed is not taken ("Not taken: press Verified if it
  works, or Reopen …"), so a conversational reply ("checking tonight") can never file work. The
  pressed Reopen is remembered in the bot's memory only: after a loopd restart, press it again, or
  use `/loop reopen`. Someone else's reply is refused.
- The thread gets one line for each: "Marked for your check once merged (you) …", "Verified by you.
  Done.", "Reopened by you: '…'. Filed #M (P2) with those words; this one is done." The new item
  gets its own thread as any new item does. A link to it is not posted: the bot has no permalink for
  a message (no workspace URL), so the line names its number.
- **The digest** lists the items in qa now ("Waiting for your check"), whatever happened in the
  window. Items waiting in qa do not, alone, cause a digest to post (that would post one every 4
  hours while something waits); they are listed whenever a digest posts.
- `/loop status` shows a `qa` column. (The web board's QA column did not, until the proofs: no lane
  read the `qa` display.)
